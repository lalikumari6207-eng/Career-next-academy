About the Program

The HDFC Bank Manager Trainee Program is a comprehensive, one-year blended learning journey that builds future branch leaders through classroom training, live branch internships and on-the-job coaching.

Graduates are trained across retail liabilities, credit, operations and team management, and are placed as Assistant Branch Managers with a clear growth path within the bank.

Program Highlights

  • 12-Month training + on-the-job program
  • 8,000+ learners placed and growing
  • Eligible for Branch Manager role in 4–5 years
  • PG Certificate in Banking & Financial Services
  • Live branch internship & sales-floor training
  • End-to-end placement assistance
